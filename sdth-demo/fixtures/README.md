# Recorded demo fixtures

The six `singapore/` missions plus three UK flights (`dji_csv_gps_jamming.csv`, `orbiter4_gps_denied_frozen.json`, `dji_csv_motor_fail_recover_024.csv`) are auto-imported into the local Mission library on Session connect. The UK three are kept for continuity with the earlier evidence and to show a non-DJI format; they live in the Stonehenge cache region, so they still get terrain in the replay.
The GPS-warning and dropout CSVs plus the Orbiter JSON are unchanged copies from the original hazard corpus and V2 Orbiter skin.
The `Exercise ·` CSVs are the audited V2 failure-plus-control set.
The two `dji_csv_supplemental_*.csv` files are independent of the original 90-file denominator.
The GPS scenario filename does not prove jamming: the supported observation is a GPS-weak warning.
