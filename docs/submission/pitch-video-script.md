**Slide 1 — Intro**

Hi, we're Team WISL.

**Slide 2 — Problem statement**

Right now, when a drone fails mid-mission, the evidence of why is scattered. It's locked
in vendor-specific formats, siloed by airframe, rarely centralised. So the same failure
can happen on five different aircraft and no one connects the dots.

**Slide 3 — Post-mission analysis is already an arms race**

This isn't hypothetical. In Ukraine, Delta's Avengers system already logs thousands of
hours of drone footage — which targets were engaged, where a strike failed, which
terminal approach actually worked. Post-mission analysis is already a serious
capability on the modern battlefield. The question isn't whether to build this. It's
whether you have it before someone else does.

**Slide 4 — What is WISL**

WISL takes thousands of hours of recorded drone telemetry and finds the specific,
repeatable failures inside it. Take GPS jamming — a large share of our sample logs
involve exactly that. Our 3D map view lets an operator see precisely where it happened,
not just that it did. From there, a fleet operator has two real options: go investigate
that location, or simply route around it next time.

**Slide 5 — AI analysis and recurring patterns**

[VIDEO — AI Analysis]

This is where it feeds forward into planning. On the simple end: if the pattern is GPS
jamming at a location, the model surfaces that, and an operator can go verify the site
before it costs them another aircraft. On the more complex end, when the same failure
signature turns up across multiple flights, WISL can surface which explanation is most
likely — giving the operator a starting point instead of a blank investigation.

One important caveat: this is a local model reasoning over recorded evidence. It's a
prediction to guide human judgement, not a certified finding.

[VIDEO — Recurring Patterns and Bulletins]

That's the same signature-matching, turned into something a maintainer can act on
directly — a bulletin, not an automated fix.

**Slide 6 — So why does this matter**

Beyond the precedent Delta has already set, this has a direct local case. Under the new
SkyGuardian initiative, WISL could help HomeTeamNS map failure hotspots in 3D over areas
like Tuas or Brani — turning those into safer planned patrol routes. And for RSAF, the
same recurrence detection applies to platform improvement — surfacing whether an error
turns up more on a Hermes 450 than a Hermes 900, for instance, as a signal worth
investigating.

**Slide 7 — Thank you**

That's WISL. Thank you.
